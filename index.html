PhD researcher in LLM evaluation & reliability

I'm a PhD student at the Institute of Formal and Applied Linguistics, working on evaluating any generated text. My thesis research is mostly focused on LLM hallucinations (no, we have not solved them) and spotting them even when no gold answer is available. I also love doing research with friends on machine translation, multilinguality, dialogue systems, and lately speech processing.

Outside of research, I like to clear my head in the gym, in nature, or on aerial hoop (google it, you will not regret it!). I also like to laugh, so if you're reading this, send me your favorite meme!
